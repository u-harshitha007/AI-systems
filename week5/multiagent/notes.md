# Notes for: What is artificial intelligence?

## Round 1 — ask_search_llm
**Task:** Provide a concise definition of artificial intelligence, including its scope and key aspects, suitable for a general audience. Include the source URL(s) of the information.

**Answer:**
**Artificial intelligence (AI)** is the field of computer science that builds systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. A widely‑cited definition (Kaplan & Haenlein, 2019) describes AI as **“a system’s ability to interpret external data, learn from it, and use that learning to accomplish specific goals and tasks through flexible adaptation.”** In everyday terms, AI includes technologies that can *perceive* their environment, *reason* about information, *learn* from data, *plan* and *make decisions*, and *understand language*—often without explicit step‑by‑step programming.

**Scope and key aspects**
- **Data interpretation & perception:** AI systems gather and analyse raw data (e.g., images, speech, sensor readings).  
- **Learning & adaptation:** Using machine‑learning techniques, they improve performance over time.  
- **Reasoning & problem‑solving:** They apply logical or statistical methods to draw conclusions and solve complex problems.  
- **Decision‑making & action:** Autonomous agents perceive their surroundings, choose actions that maximise the chance of achieving defined goals, and adapt as conditions change.  
- **Broad applicability:** AI spans narrow (task‑specific) systems (e.g., voice assistants), general AI concepts, and emerging super‑intelligent research, affecting domains such as healthcare, finance, transportation, and everyday consumer products.
